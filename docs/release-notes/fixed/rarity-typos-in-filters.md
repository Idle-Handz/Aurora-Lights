The rarity filters in the Shop and Compendium now offer only the real rarities plus Varies (rarity that depends on something or spans several, such as "Rarity Varies" or "Rare, Very Rare, or Legendary"), Infusion (artificer infusions) and Unknown (anything else), and a rarity that is one typo away from a real one, such as "Vert Rare", "Lgendary" or "unommon", is read as the rarity it was meant to be.
